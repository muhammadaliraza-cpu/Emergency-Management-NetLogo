## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an agent-based simulation of emergency management during a water contamination event, developed using NetLogo. It explores the interactions between environmental contamination dynamics, individual behavioral responses, and coordinated emergency interventions.

The simulation incorporates multiple agents, including the general public, government authorities, volunteers, and healthcare providers. By modeling their interactions under varying environmental and operational conditions, the project provides a framework for investigating emergency preparedness, evacuation strategies, and resource coordination.

The primary objective is to explore how contamination propagation, public reaction times, and emergency response interventions influence evacuation and casualty outcomes.

## 👥 Simulation Agents

| Agent | Role and Behavior |
|---|---|
| 🔵 General Public | Respond to contamination exposure and initiate evacuation based on environmental conditions and reaction delays. |
| 🔴 Government | Issues emergency alerts to reduce reaction times and facilitate timely evacuation. |
| 🟢 Volunteers | Assist nearby individuals in reaching safe areas during emergencies. |
| 🩺 Healthcare Providers | Provide assistance and rescue affected individuals. |

## ⚙️ Model Features

- Spatial modeling of environmental contamination and exposure
- Contamination propagation influenced by wind and water flow
- Individual evacuation decisions and reaction delays
- Government emergency alerts and public response coordination
- Volunteer-assisted evacuation mechanisms
- Healthcare intervention and casualty management
- Real-time monitoring of evacuation and casualty statistics
- Adjustable simulation parameters for scenario exploration

## 🔬 Research Objectives

This simulation aims to investigate:

1. How environmental conditions influence contamination propagation.
2. How individual response delays affect evacuation outcomes.
3. How government alerts improve emergency preparedness and response.
4. How volunteers and healthcare providers influence emergency outcomes.
5. How alternative resource allocation and response strategies affect simulation results.

## 🚀 How to Run the Model

1. Install [NetLogo](https://ccl.northwestern.edu/netlogo/).
2. Clone or download this GitHub repository.
3. Open the `.nlogo` or `.nlogox` model file using NetLogo.
4. Adjust simulation parameters, including population size, volunteers, healthcare personnel, and contamination spread rate.
5. Click `setup` to initialize the model.
6. Click `go` to start the simulation.
7. Observe contamination propagation, agent behavior, evacuation progress, and casualty outcomes.
